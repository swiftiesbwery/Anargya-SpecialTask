Who we are

Anargya is a student research team at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) Surabaya, under PUI-SKO, the Center of Excellence for Automotive Control Systems. The team designs and builds electric formula race cars and competes with them internationally.

Members come from many ITS departments, working across mechanical, electrical, and non-technical divisions to turn a design into a race car.

Our history

Since 2018, Anargya has grown from a small student research team at ITS into a regular competitor at FSAE Japan, FSAE Australasia, and Formula Bharat. Here is how it happened, one car at a time.

2018Founded

The team is founded

Anargya starts as a student research team at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) Surabaya, under PUI-SKO, the Center of Excellence for Automotive Control Systems, with one goal: to design, build, and race electric formula cars.

2019Mark 1.0

The first car and the first race

The first Anargya Formula EV, Mark 1.0, was launched at the ITS Rectorate with a 72 kW motor and a control system from ITS research. It then went to Student Formula Japan and finished 10th best EV at FSAE Japan 2019.

2021Mark 2.0

A second generation

In November 2021 ITS grand-launched Mark 2.0 at Graha Sepuluh Nopember, aimed at FSAE Japan 2022 with a tubular frame, better motor and battery efficiency, and a more aerodynamic body.

2023Mark 3.0

On the podium in Japan

Racing Mark 3.0 with a battery raised to 7.46 kWh, the team came home from FSAE Japan 2023 with 3rd place in the Presentation Event and 3rd for Best Vehicle Drawing.

2024Mark 4.0

The breakthrough year

Mark 4.0 was launched in Surabaya on 20 August 2024. The team then ranked 1st among South East Asian teams at FSAE Japan 2024, took 2nd in the EV class among overseas teams and 3rd in the Presentation Event, and won Overall at Formula Bharat Pi-EV 2024. Earlier that year it also showed the Mark 3.0 at GIIAS 2024.

2025Mark 5.0

Going international

At FSAE Japan 2025 the team won the PR Awards and placed 2nd in the Presentation Event. On 28 November 2025 it launched Mark 5.0, with an upgraded powertrain, to race at Calder Park Raceway in Australia.

2026Mark 6.0

Three titles in Aichi

Mark 6.0 had its grand launch on 26 June 2026 and debuted at FSAE Japan 2026 at Aichi Sky Expo, winning 1st in Three-View Drawing (EV), 2nd in MathWorks Modelling (EV), and 2nd in the PR Awards.
