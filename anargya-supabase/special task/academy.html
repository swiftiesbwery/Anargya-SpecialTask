What is it

What is Anargya Academy?

Anargya Academy isn’t just a workshop.

It is a free opportunity for ITS students to get to know Anargya. The event features comprehensive training and workshops conducted by our technical divisions, covering both Mechanical and Electrical aspects, designed to introduce you to our work and initiatives.

You sharpen real skills through hands-on training with the Anargya ITS EV Team, the student team behind the electric formula cars that compete in Formula SAE Japan.
