Electric Formula Research Team

Anargya is the student research and development team of Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) Surabaya. We design, build, and race electric formula cars, and have represented Indonesia at FSAE Japan, FSAE Australasia, and Formula Bharat.

MARK 5.0

Formula EV  |  FSAE Australasia 2025

About the car

Mark 5.0 is Anargya's fifth-generation Formula EV, launched on 28 November 2025 to race at Calder Park Raceway in Australia. Development focused on the powertrain: an Emrax 208 Medium Voltage motor paired with a lithium-ion battery, built on a tubular space-frame chassis.

MARK 6.0

Formula EV  |  FSAE Japan 2026

About the car

Mark 6.0 is the newest generation, developed with a more mature design and modelling approach. It raced as car E21 at FSAE Japan 2026 in Aichi Sky Expo, and supports clean energy, industry innovation, and climate action goals.
